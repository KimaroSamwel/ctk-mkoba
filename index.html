📣 TAARIFA MUHIMU KWA WANACHAMA WOTE — SEPTEMBA 2026

Kuanzia Septemba 2026 kikundi kinaanza rasmi mpango wa uwekezaji uliopitishwa na uongozi. Kwa mujibu wa makubaliano ya mwisho:

  • Wanachama wasio na deni au wenye deni la 2024–2026 wanaotaka kuendelea watatakiwa kujibu na kuonyesha lini watalipa angalau 75% ya deni lao kabla ya mwisho wa Desemba 2026.
  • Wanachama wasiotaka kuendelea, wasiojibu, au ambao deni lao halijapangwa kufutiwa watatolewa mwezi Septemba 2026.
  • Kwa wale wanaobaki na deni: faida yao ya uwekezaji itatumika kufidia deni kabla ya kulipwa.
  • Faini rasmi za kuchelewa zitaanza kutekelezwa Januari 2027 (TZS 10,000 kwa kuchelewa zaidi ya miezi miwili mfululizo).

Tafadhali soma Taarifa kamili hapa chini. Wasiliana na Katibu (Pendo Malangwa) au Mwenyekiti (Samwel Kimaro) kupitia WhatsApp kwa maelezo zaidi.

Kiswahili

KWA WANACHAMA WOTE WA CTK MKOBA 2012, Tunawashukuru kwa ushirikiano wenu wote wakati wote huu. Kutokana na maamuzi yaliyofikiwa katika kikao cha mwisho, tayari tunaanza mpango rasmi wa UWEKEZAJI kikundi kuanzia Septemba 2026. Kwa sababu hiyo na kutaka kuhakikisha kikundi kinakaa imara na kuwanufaisha wote, maazimio yafuatayo yanatekelezwa kuanzia Septemba 2026: 1. UONDOLEWAJI WA WANACHAMA WASIOFANYA KAZI - Mwanachama ambaye haimalizi deni lake la 2024–2026 NAE HATAKI KUENDELEA ataondolewa kwenye kikundi Septemba 2026. - Mwanachama ANATAKA KUENDELEA anatakiwa kujibu kwa uongozi (Samwel, Pendo au Vanessa) na kuonyesha LITAKAPO LIPA ANGAU 75% YA DENI LAKE KABLA YA MWISHO WA MWAKA (Desemba 2026). - Mwanachama aliye na deni na asiyejibu ahadi yake: FAIDA YAKE YA UWEKEZAJI ITATUMIKA KUFIDIA DENI. 2. FAINI - Huduma ya faini itaanza kufanya kazi kwa ukamilifu JANUARI 2027, kwa mujibu wa katiba (TZS 10,000 kwa kila mwezi wa kuchelewa zaidi ya miezi miwili). 3. UWEKEZAJI - Kuanzia Septemba 2026 sehemu ya michango itawekezwa (mfano 30%) kwenye mfuko uliosajiliwa na CMSA au Hati za Serikali. Faida itagawanywa kwa wanachama kwa uwiano wa michango zao. Tafadhali mjibu kwa viongozi kabla ya mwisho wa Septemba 2026, kuthibitisha kama mnaendelea na kuonyesha mpango wa kulipa deni. Baraka kwa wote. UONGOZI WA CTK MKOBA 2012

English

TO ALL CTK MKOBA 2012 MEMBERS, Thank you for your continued cooperation. Following the decisions at our last meeting, the group officially starts its INVESTMENT program in September 2026. To keep the group strong and ensure everyone benefits, the following resolutions take effect from September 2026: 1. REMOVAL OF INACTIVE MEMBERS - A member who has not cleared their 2024–2026 debt AND does NOT wish to continue will be removed from the group in September 2026. - A member who WISHES TO CONTINUE must reply to the leadership (Samwel, Pendo or Vanessa) stating WHEN they will clear at least 75% of their debt before year-end (December 2026). - For a member who remains in debt without honoring their commitment: their investment returns will be applied to clear the debt. 2. FINES - The fine system becomes fully effective JANUARY 2027, per the constitution (TZS 10,000 for each month of delay beyond two months). 3. INVESTMENT - From September 2026, a portion of monthly contributions (e.g. 30%) will be invested in a CMSA-licensed fund or Government Securities. Returns will be shared among members in proportion to their contributions. Please respond to the leadership before the end of September 2026 to confirm whether you are continuing and to state your debt-clearance plan. CTK MKOBA 2012 LEADERSHIP
